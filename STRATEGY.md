# TSL Logistics — Nigeria National Resilience Twin

## Executive proposition

TSL Logistics should position itself as Nigeria’s sovereign delivery and lifecycle integrator for a federated homeland-security resilience platform. The offer should strengthen existing public programmes and statutory owners rather than create a duplicate command structure.

KONGSBERG is the Norwegian technology anchor for integrated maritime surveillance, hydrography, autonomous underwater survey, subsea inspection and maritime decision support. Exail is the advanced-technology anchor for supervised autonomous surface systems, inertial navigation, subsea positioning, sonar and remote hydrographic operations. TSL owns the Nigerian layer: programme integration, infrastructure operations, logistics, HSSEQ, data stewardship, maintenance, local workforce transfer, service levels and benefits realisation.

This is a defensive, civilian-protection and critical-infrastructure strategy. It does not include weapons, targeting, precise vulnerability mapping, personal tracking, predictive policing or autonomous enforcement.

## Why this fits TSL

TSL’s documented capabilities include petroleum terminal operations, pipeline management and surveillance, inventory-management technology, infrastructure development, HSSEQ, relationship management and supply-chain consultancy. That gives TSL a credible bridge between technology procurement and sustained Nigerian operations.

The commercial differentiation is not a reseller relationship. It is an accountable managed-service model in which TSL integrates OEM capabilities, runs local lifecycle support and is measured against public outcomes.

## First 90 days

### Workstream A — mandate and baseline

- Appoint executive sponsor and cross-agency steering body.
- Define two public outcomes, owners and baseline measures.
- Catalogue existing systems and interfaces before buying new platforms.
- Complete legal-basis, DPIA, cyber and human-rights screening.

### Workstream B — NNS LANA and hydrography

- Conduct OEM-led technical health and obsolescence review.
- Verify KONGSBERG EM2040/EM304 and wider survey-stack readiness.
- Assess crewing, spares, software, calibration, data processing and chart workflow.
- Design a DriX service demonstration that complements, rather than displaces, the national survey fleet.

### Workstream C — two lighthouse pilots

1. Bounded maritime/hydrographic data-fusion and survey-productivity pilot.
2. Critical-infrastructure integrity twin on a consenting TSL-managed asset.

Each pilot must define: outcome, owner, lawful data, safety case, architecture, training, baseline, KPI, independent evaluator, stop conditions, scale conditions and exit plan.

## Roadmap

- Phase 0, 0–90 days: mandate, discovery, baseline and pilot design.
- Phase 1, 3–9 months: run two lighthouse services and independent evaluation.
- Phase 2, 9–24 months: sovereign exchange, knowledge graph, zonal integration and local lifecycle centre.
- Phase 3, 24–48 months: scale validated services nationally with annual exercises and audit.
- Phase 4, 48+ months: Nigeria-owned capability and regional service export.

## Commercial model

- Paid discovery with fixed deliverables.
- Fixed-outcome pilots rather than open-ended equipment trials.
- Modular framework agreement by mission service.
- Managed-service fees tied to availability, data quality, survey throughput, asset readiness, local capacity and safeguards.
- Lease/service options for autonomous platforms where ownership is not initially economical.
- Open-interface, data-export and transition-assistance clauses.
- Nigeria-controlled encryption keys and source-system authority.
- Local-content milestones tied to training, certification, maintenance and configuration capability.

No unvalidated budget figures are included. A cost envelope should follow technical discovery, market engagement and acquisition strategy.

## Governance non-negotiables

1. Mission before data collection.
2. Federated source authority and minimum-necessary sharing.
3. Named human accountability for consequential decisions.
4. No predictive policing or sensitive-trait inference.
5. Provenance, timestamp, transformation and confidence for every material claim.
6. Purpose- and attribute-based access with regular recertification.
7. Time-bound retention, legal hold and verified deletion.
8. Independent oversight, algorithm register, complaints and redress.
9. Nigerian sovereignty, open interfaces and exit rights.
10. Zero-trust security, segmentation, secure updates, backups and tested recovery.

## Board-level KPIs

- Mission datasets with owner, provenance, timestamp and confidence.
- Median time from verified referral to acknowledged ownership.
- Participating critical assets with current inspection and recovery plans.
- Priority hydrographic survey area accepted through national QA.
- SAR/navigation cases supported by a shared, auditable picture.
- Contracted service availability and recovery performance.
- Certified Nigerian personnel able to operate and maintain each service.
- Access-control breaches, complaints and redress closure time.
- Priority interfaces passing conformance tests.
- Audited avoided downtime, vessel days and duplicate procurement.
